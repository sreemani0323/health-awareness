💡 PRACTICAL HEALTH TIP

Lather Friction Removes Microbes

Rinsing alone does not remove oily dirt and pathogens. Scrubbing with soap for at least 20 seconds creates the mechanical friction needed to loosen microbes from between fingers, backs of hands, and under nails.
