# HealthAware Learner Experience — Usability Test Plan & Field Observation Protocol

**Target Group**: Caregivers, parents, adolescents, and community members in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.  
**Context**: Shared family smartphones, modest Android devices, 3G/4G connectivity, bilingual English-Telugu households (testing the English platform interface).  
**Purpose**: Assess usability, clarity, engagement, and retention mechanisms before formal field trials.

---

## 1. Study Overview & Ethical Boundaries

- **Voluntary Participation**: Learners and families participate voluntarily; no personal identification, contact details, or children's personal records are collected.
- **Device Privacy**: All progress tracking is stored locally in the browser (`localStorage`). No server tracking of individuals is performed.
- **Educational Scope**: Tests evaluate comprehension and usability of public health guidance; no medical diagnoses or therapeutic claims are made.

## 3. Seven Core Task Scenarios

### Task 1: Discover a Relevant Lesson from the Homepage
- **Prompt**: "Imagine you want to learn how to keep your family healthy by properly washing hands. Starting from the homepage, find and open that lesson."
- **Success Criteria**: Participant navigates from hero starting points ("Explore Health Topics" or "Start This Lesson" featured card) to the handwashing lesson without confusion.

### Task 2: Complete One Interactive Lesson Independently
- **Prompt**: "Go through the lesson cards until you reach the end of the lesson."
- **Success Criteria**: Participant uses "Continue" and step indicators to move through explanation, quiz, and recap cards without external assistance.

### Task 3: Answer a Knowledge-Check Question
- **Prompt**: "On the quiz card, select the answer you believe is correct and check your response."
- **Success Criteria**: Participant understands radio selections, checks the answer, reviews feedback, and uses "Try Again" if incorrect.

### Task 4: Find the Health Source Behind a Recommendation
- **Prompt**: "Where can you see the official medical source or guideline that supports this lesson?"
- **Success Criteria**: Participant identifies and expands the "View Verified Medical Sources" panel, locating WHO / CDC references.

### Task 5: Complete a Practical Family Activity ("Try This at Home")
- **Prompt**: "Find a practical activity you can practice at home with family members, and mark it as completed on your phone."
- **Success Criteria**: Participant locates either the lesson completion takeaway or the homepage "Try This at Home" section and taps "Mark as Practiced Today".

### Task 6: Return to an Unfinished Lesson (Resume Learning)
- **Prompt**: "Close your browser or go back to the homepage. How would you resume where you left off?"
- **Success Criteria**: Participant notices the "Saved Progress: Continue Learning" card in the hero, verifies card step indicator, and resumes with one tap.

### Task 7: Share a Lesson Link with a Caregiver or Neighbor
- **Prompt**: "If you wanted to share this lesson with a family member or friend, how would you send them the link?"
- **Success Criteria**: Participant taps "Share Lesson", notices confirmation toast, and understands the public URL is copied to clipboard / shared.

## 5. Post-Session Semi-Structured Questions

1. **Clarity**: "Were the instructions for the home activities easy to follow, or did anything feel unsafe or impractical for your household?"
2. **Engagement**: "Did the interactive questions help you understand the health advice, or did they feel like a school test?"
3. **Retention**: "What would make you or your family want to return to HealthAware next week?"
4. **Length**: "Did the lessons feel too long, too short, or just right for viewing on a phone during a break?"
5. **Shared Device Experience**: "Was it clear that your progress was saved only on this phone? Would you feel comfortable clearing it if sharing with someone else?"
